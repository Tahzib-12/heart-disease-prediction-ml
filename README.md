## 1. Project Overview
Coronary artery disease remains one of the primary causes of cardiovascular morbidity worldwide. The objective of this project is to build a transparent, reproducible, and explainable machine learning pipeline that predicts the presence or absence of heart disease using non-invasive clinical and laboratory indicators from the authentic **UCI Heart Disease (Cleveland) Database**.

Designed specifically for undergraduate academic evaluation, the project emphasizes:
- **High Explainability & Standard Design Patterns:** Avoids black-box complexity and focuses on Scikit-Learn pipelines.
- **Strict Zero-Leakage Policy:** Feature scaling, imputation, and categorical encoding are fitted solely on training splits.
- **Rigorous Model Selection via Stratified CV:** Models are compared using 5-fold Stratified Cross-Validation on the training data; the test set is kept completely untouched until final evaluation.
- **Genuine Executed Results:** All metrics, confusion matrices, and ROC curves reflect actual executed experimental runs.

## 4. Dataset Specification & Preprocessing

- **Dataset Source:** Official UCI Machine Learning Repository — Heart Disease Dataset (Cleveland database)  
  *Citation:* Janosi, A., Steinbrunn, W., Pfisterer, W., & Detrano, R. (1989). Heart Disease. UCI Machine Learning Repository. https://doi.org/10.24432/C52P4X
- **Total Instances:** 303 patients
- **Total Features:** 13 clinical predictor variables + 1 target variable
- **Missing Value Handling:** Missing values (`?` in raw text) occur in two discrete attributes: `ca` (4 missing instances) and `thal` (2 missing instances). These represent ~2% of rows. Rather than discarding samples, they are imputed inside the Scikit-Learn pipeline.
- **Target Transformation:**
  - Original `num = 0` $\rightarrow$ **0 (Heart Disease Absent / < 50% narrowing)**
  - Original `num = 1, 2, 3, 4` $\rightarrow$ **1 (Heart Disease Present / > 50% narrowing)**
  - Class balance: 164 Absent (54.12%), 139 Present (45.88%).

### Clinical Feature Typing
| Feature Name | Clinical Meaning | Feature Type | Preprocessing Strategy |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `age` | Age in years (29 – 77) | Continuous | SimpleImputer(strategy='median') $\rightarrow$ StandardScaler() |
| `trestbps` | Resting blood pressure on hospital admission (mm Hg) | Continuous | SimpleImputer(strategy='median') $\rightarrow$ StandardScaler() |
| `chol` | Serum cholesterol (mg/dl) | Continuous | SimpleImputer(strategy='median') $\rightarrow$ StandardScaler() |
| `thalach` | Maximum heart rate achieved during exercise stress test | Continuous | SimpleImputer(strategy='median') $\rightarrow$ StandardScaler() |

## 5. Model Comparison & Experimental Results

Four diverse machine learning algorithms were trained and validated:
1. **Logistic Regression:** `max_iter=1000, random_state=42`
2. **K-Nearest Neighbors (KNN):** `n_neighbors=5`
3. **Decision Tree Classifier:** `max_depth=4, random_state=42`
4. **Random Forest Classifier:** `n_estimators=100, max_depth=5, random_state=42`

### 5-Fold Stratified Cross-Validation on Training Data ($N=242$)
*(Sorted by primary selection criterion: Mean CV ROC-AUC)*

| Model | Mean CV ROC-AUC | Mean CV Recall | Mean CV F1-Score | Mean CV Accuracy | Mean CV Precision |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **Logistic Regression** | **0.9025 (±0.0144)** | **0.7834 (±0.0459)** | **0.8245 (±0.0069)** | **0.8471 (±0.0100)** | **0.8768 (±0.0542)** |
| **Random Forest** | 0.8927 (±0.0293) | 0.7644 (±0.0983) | 0.7905 (±0.0485) | 0.8180 (±0.0309) | 0.8281 (±0.0309) |
| **K-Nearest Neighbors** | 0.8712 (±0.0387) | 0.7826 (±0.0893) | 0.7989 (±0.0426) | 0.8222 (±0.0288) | 0.8232 (±0.0311) |
| **Decision Tree** | 0.7863 (±0.0265) | 0.6743 (±0.0861) | 0.6922 (±0.0578) | 0.7273 (±0.0441) | 0.7171 (±0.0621) |

### Model Selection Rationale
- **Primary Metric:** Logistic Regression achieved the highest Mean CV ROC-AUC (0.9025) with the lowest standard deviation (±0.0144), indicating superior stability across folds.
- **Supporting Metrics:** It also achieved the top Mean CV Recall (0.7834) and F1-score (0.8245).
- **Parsimony:** For a small clinical dataset ($N=303$), a regularized linear model minimizes variance and prevents the overfitting tendencies observed in unconstrained tree-based models.

## 7. Metric Interpretation & Medical Screening Trade-Offs

In medical screening contexts, classification errors do not carry symmetric costs:

1. **The Critical Importance of Recall (Sensitivity):**
   - A **False Negative (FN)** represents a patient with active coronary artery disease who is misdiagnosed as healthy and sent home without care. This can lead to myocardial infarction or sudden death.
   - A **False Positive (FP)** results in secondary diagnostic testing (e.g. stress echocardiography, angiogram). While it incurs monetary and emotional stress, it does not pose an immediate fatal hazard.
   - Therefore, in disease screening, **Recall takes priority over Accuracy alone**. Our model attained **92.86% Recall**, successfully capturing 26 out of 28 diseased individuals and yielding only 2 false negatives.

2. **The Role of ROC-AUC (0.9665):**
   - ROC-AUC measures the probability that the model ranks a randomly chosen disease-positive patient higher than a randomly chosen healthy patient. A score of 0.9665 confirms outstanding discriminatory capacity across all diagnostic thresholds.

## 10. Comprehensive Viva & Oral Examination Guide

### Q1: Why did you convert the target `num` into a binary variable?
**Answer:** The original UCI Cleveland dataset records angiographic status from 0 (<50% diameter narrowing) to 1, 2, 3, and 4 (>50% narrowing across 1 to 4 coronary vessels). In medical screening, the primary clinical objective is to determine whether significant coronary disease is present or absent to decide whether further intervention is needed. Converting 0 to negative and 1–4 to positive standardizes the problem as binary classification.

### Q2: What is data leakage and how did your pipeline prevent it?
**Answer:** Data leakage occurs when information from outside the training dataset (such as test set distributions) is used during feature scaling or imputation, giving the model an unrealistic performance boost. We prevented leakage by performing the 80/20 train/test split **first**, and encapsulating all imputers and scalers inside Scikit-Learn's `ColumnTransformer` and `Pipeline`. All parameters (such as medians, modes, and standard deviation scalers) were learned **solely on `X_train`** during cross-validation.

### Q3: Why is ROC-AUC preferred over Accuracy for model selection?
**Answer:** Accuracy is threshold-dependent (assuming a default cut-off of 0.5) and can be misleading even in mildly imbalanced distributions. ROC-AUC evaluates the classifier's discriminative ability across all possible operating thresholds by integrating the True Positive Rate against the False Positive Rate. It provides a robust, threshold-independent measure of how well positive and negative risk scores are separated.

### Q4: Why did Logistic Regression outperform Random Forest and Decision Trees here?
**Answer:** The Cleveland dataset has 303 samples and 13 features. Complex non-linear models like Decision Trees and deep Random Forests have high capacity and can easily overfit subtle noise in small sample sets. In contrast, regularized Logistic Regression has lower variance and excels when continuous clinical features exhibit smooth, monotonic relationships with disease risk.

### Q5: What is the difference between Sensitivity and Specificity?
**Answer:**
- **Sensitivity (Recall = $\frac{TP}{TP + FN}$):** The probability that the test correctly identifies a patient who truly has heart disease.
- **Specificity ($\frac{TN}{TN + FP}$):** The probability that the test correctly identifies a patient who is free of heart disease.

### Q6: How are missing values handled in your project?
**Answer:** The raw UCI file encodes missing entries as `'?'`. We parse these as `NaN`. There are 4 missing values in `ca` and 2 in `thal`. We use `SimpleImputer(strategy='most_frequent')` inside the categorical pipeline branch so that the mode of the training partition imputes missing discrete entries during both training and inference.
